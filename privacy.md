# Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026.

## Data we use

SyncNuke processes your chosen username, room details, media filename, and playback state
only to synchronize playback. An optional room password is used to join protected rooms.

SyncNuke does not collect IP addresses or device fingerprints as synchronization data.

## Sharing

This information is sent to the synchronization service; your username, media filename,
and playback state are shared with other participants in the room.

## Retention

Participant information is removed from the active room when you disconnect. The DataSaver
server keeps room playback state, including the media filename, in memory for the duration of a session.
Other server operators may have different retention practices.
